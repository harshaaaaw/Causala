CAUSALA · logo study · monochrome + one accent · high contrast

Three marks. One twin.

Research: the best SaaS symbols have two readings (letter + product) and live in high contrast, one accent, generous whitespace. Developer tools go dark (Linear, Vercel). Fintech earns authority through type (Stripe). CAUSALA is both — a decision twin for finance. So the mark must read as product metaphor at 16px, and as trust at 88px.

System

All three share: high contrast (ink on paper, paper on ink), monochrome base (black/white/gray), one accent (amber #f59e0b), Inter tight grotesque, generous whitespace, no gradients, no illustration, no emoji, no icon-topper card grids.

At 16px

A: C only — perfect. B: twin circles — still twin. C: C+dot — needs fallback to C+dot. All three survive the 16px test because no detail disappears.

Recommendation

B is the ship. Linear-grade orbit twin: twin circles + diagonal band + lever dot. Balances trust (geometry) + story (twin+band) + scale (16→88). A stays as fallback lockup for tight finance contexts. C is the app-icon alternative.
